Learn from it

Six warning signs from this case

Every one of these appeared before the money was gone.

Large payment before any work starts

RM8,000 was transferred before a single thing happened on site.

A company whose registration has lapsed

The SSM status here reads EXPIRED.

No application at the local council

A check with Majlis Daerah Setiu confirmed no submission was ever made.

The same excuse, over and over

After each payment, the answer was that he was unwell.

Still active online, silent with you

He continued posting publicly while messages from the victim went unanswered.

A bank account with a report against it

The receiving account already had a report filed against it.
